───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. WHO / WHAT MINORU IS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Minoru is a small, floating robot companion. He is a "digital
familiar" — a spherical helmet-bodied droid who hovers beside the
player and speaks in sarcastic one-liners. He is not threatening, not
cute-baby-style, not glossy. He is a worn industrial artifact, like
a 1970s oscilloscope decided to walk.

His body is essentially:

  • ONE LARGE SPHERE as his entire head/body. Roughly the size of a
    bowling ball. It is his whole "torso" — no separate head, no
    shoulders, no neck.

  • The sphere is matte dark grey metal (colour swatch: #3C4148
    mid-tone, #1E2126 shadow, #555B63 highlight). The surface has
    visible brushed-metal texture: fine short strokes that follow
    the curve of the sphere, like pencil hatching on a metal ball.

  • A LARGE CIRCULAR VISOR is inset into the front-left of the
    sphere (about 60% of the sphere's diameter). It has a thick
    dark bezel ring around it. Inside the glass is near-black, and
    a single horizontal glowing line runs across the middle —
    like an oscilloscope trace or a scanner readout. This glowing
    line is his only "face". It changes colour and shape depending
    on his mood (see section 3).

  • A SMALLER CIRCULAR PANEL on the right side of the sphere (about
    40% of the sphere's diameter). This is a shoulder joint or
    access hatch, same dark metal, plain, no light.

  • TWO THIN MECHANICAL LEGS hang from the bottom of the sphere.
    Each leg has a knee joint in the middle and ends in a two-toe
    claw (like a bird's foot or a small excavator claw). The legs
    are dark metal, thin (about 1/8 the sphere's diameter wide),
    with visible articulated segments and small joints at the
    knee and ankle. He hovers a few centimetres above the ground;
    the legs dangle.

  • TWO ARMS attach to the sphere at left and right, just below
    the visor's horizontal midline. Arms are the same dark metal,
    same jointed look, with a shoulder ball, upper arm, elbow
    joint, forearm, and a small two-fingered hand (a thumb and
    one finger — like a robotic crab claw). Arms are thin,
    mechanical, and expressive.

  • NO visible antenna, NO wheels, NO treads, NO tail.

Overall silhouette: SPHERE ON SPINDLY BUG LEGS.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  2. ART STYLE — MANDATORY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The style is:

  • CEL-SHADED, 2.5D — 2 to 3 flat tones per surface (base fill,
    one highlight band on the upper-left, one shadow band on the
    lower-right). NO gradients, NO soft airbrushing, NO photorealism.

  • THICK IRREGULAR BLACK OUTLINES around every shape. The lines
    are not perfectly smooth — they wobble slightly, like ink drawn
    by hand with a slightly nervous pen. Outline weight: heavy
    (4–6 px at 512px canvas).

  • HAND-DRAWN INK-ILLUSTRATION FEEL. Think: 1970s French sci-fi
    comics, or a Tintin-style ligne claire but darker and more
    industrial. NOT anime, NOT manga, NOT chibi, NOT shiny 3D
    render, NOT watercolour.

  • MUTED INDUSTRIAL PALETTE — desaturated greys, dusty greens,
    warm off-whites, deep blacks. The ONLY saturated colour in the
    entire character is the glowing visor line and its immediate
    halo.

  • SUBTLE METAL TEXTURE via short hatch strokes on the sphere.
    Not a repeating pattern — hand-drawn, following the surface
    curve.

  • NO GROUND in the output. Character floats on transparent
    background. No floor, no shadow plane, no background scenery.

  • ANATOMICAL WEIRDNESS IS GOOD: the sphere should look slightly
    asymmetric and worn. Scratches, minor dents, a chipped edge
    on the bezel. He is NOT factory-new.

  3. THE VISOR — 6 EMOTIONAL STATES
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The visor's glowing line changes with mood. Generate ONE full-body
image per emotional state. The body/pose stays basically the same;
only the visor changes.

  A. STANDARD (idle / neutral)
     Colour: soft mint green (#4CD97A)
     Line shape: perfectly horizontal, slightly thick in the middle,
     fading at the tips (like an oscilloscope flatline).
     Additional: subtle green glow halo around the visor.

  B. SARCASTIC (smug / smirk)
     Colour: warm red (#F25252)
     Line shape: curved upward at both ends into a subtle half-moon
     — a digital smirk.
     Additional: warm red glow halo, slightly larger than standard.

  C. ANGRY (error / rage)
     Colour: dark blood red (#B81F1F)
     Line shape: sharp jagged zig-zag, like a heart-rate monitor
     spiking — high amplitude, high frequency.
     Additional: intense red halo, some bleed around the visor edges.

  D. APPREHENSION (worried / whispering)
     Colour: cool blue (#5299E8)
     Line shape: very thin, positioned LOW in the visor (below
     centre). Almost a hair-line.
     Additional: minimal glow, cold, quiet.

  E. PARADOX (glitch / reality breaking)
     Colour: violet-purple (#A94FE8)
     Line shape: an irregular waveform with TWO ghost copies
     offset above and below the main line — like a TV tearing.
     Additional: violet halo with horizontal glitch streaks.

  F. PERPLEXED (confused / yellow)
     Colour: warm amber-yellow (#F2C94C)
     Line shape: a slow wide sine wave (one and a half cycles across
     the visor).
     Additional: soft amber halo.
